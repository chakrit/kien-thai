# Repo once-over — 2026-11-13

Read-only review of the repo at commit `3695c88` (last activity 2026-07-27), after a
four-month pause. The review ran before the gitignored corpus and iteration 11–15
outputs were synced to the reviewing machine, so corpus quotes and iteration-15 outputs
were not inspected. Findings came from three parallel review passes (methodology,
ruleset, tooling); the load-bearing ones were spot-checked by hand. Anything about Thai
naturalness is marked **needs native ear** — no finding here is a Thai-quality verdict.

## What the repo is

- **The artifact:** two agent skills. `skills/kien-thai/` is a ruleset (7 "frames",
  ai-tells, grammar, craft, style-rules, register, examples, exemplars, forbidden
  phrases) for Thai prose that does not read as AI output, has a human voice, and resists
  the over-formal Thai that training data skews toward. `skills/kode-thai/` is the audit
  loop: draft, audit against kien-thai, fix, repeat until the auditor reports `CLEAN`.
- **The bench:** a pytest harness (`tests/`) that prepends a register-scoped bundle to
  claude / codex CLI calls, runs the loop, and writes per-iteration outputs under
  `workspace/iteration-N/`. A Typhoon (Thai-native, via ollama) draft arm and a
  comparison page were added in June–July.
- **The judge:** chakrit's ear only. No LLM judge. `skill-clean` (auditor finds
  nothing) is explicitly *not* quality; the measurement is the gap to `chakrit-clean`.
- **Direction on record:** exemplar-first (2026-05-30) — native before/after pairs are
  the default artifact, rules only when a pair does not transfer, and the
  rules:exemplars ratio should invert. Revised 2026-07-26: a Thai-native model is the
  preferred *drafter* where reachable; kien-thai becomes the audit-and-voice layer. That
  revision is conditional on the iteration-15 ear review, which has not happened.
- **Where it stalled:** every thread waits on the ear. Last ear session 2026-07-07.
